What I do

Seven tasks people come to me with most often

Real estate, shops

Price and market tracker

Problem: competitor prices and new listings are tracked by hand.

Solution: a program collects prices and listings from the sites you choose, compares them and sends the result to Telegram or a spreadsheet.

Whether your exact sites can be scraped, we check in the free call.

Factories, enterprises

Equipment automation

Problem: readings (temperature, vibration, counters) are written down by hand, and failures are noticed late.

Solution: I collect sensor data over MQTT (Mosquitto, paho-mqtt), keep a log and send an alert when something leaves the normal range.

Fits if your machine or device supports MQTT: I connect and configure it. For simple sensors I have basic C/C++ knowledge for microcontrollers. Demo with a live dashboard.

Studios, shops, schools

Content without the daily routine

Problem: posts and videos take an hour every day.

Solution: a service takes an event, product or news item, AI writes the text and builds the image or video, and you just approve it or let it publish automatically.

Example: the news channel below.

Schools, studios, freelancers

CRM and records

Problem: clients, schedules and subscriptions live in a notebook or Excel.

Solution: a web system with clients, a clash-free schedule, lesson balances and Telegram reminders.

Examples: TutorDesk, Music Studio CRM.

Online sales, services

Payments and integrations

Problem: payments are confirmed by hand and your tools don't talk to each other.

Solution: online payments through Stripe, automatic emails and receipts, Telegram bots, connecting your site to other services.

Example: Movie Store API.

Telegram first; other messengers (Viber, WhatsApp) we can discuss on the call.

Any small business

Solution: a web service with a client account, bookings, payments and an admin panel for you.

Main example

A news channel that runs itself

An automated news channel for Ukrainians in Czechia. Every day it picks what matters, makes the videos and publishes them. I built it myself, from news collection to publishing.

The code of this project is private, but on a call I'll show the admin panel and explain how it works.

See the channel on Telegram

  1. Collects news from seven Czech outlets plus public holidays
  2. AI picks the topics that matter to Ukrainians in Czechia
  3. Writes the script and finds photos or quotes
  4. Records the voice-over in Ukrainian and builds subtitles
  5. Renders a vertical video with animation and music
  6. Publishes to TikTok and YouTube Shorts, shorter news as Telegram posts
  7. Admin panel: schedule, autopilot, AI cost tracking
